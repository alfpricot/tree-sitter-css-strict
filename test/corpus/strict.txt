Line comments after a rule are errors
=======================================

a {} // x

---

  (rule_set
    (selectors
      (tag_name))
    (block))
  (ERROR
    (UNEXPECTED 'x')))

---

  (rule_set
    (selectors
      (tag_name))

        (property_name)

        (UNEXPECTED 'x')))))
